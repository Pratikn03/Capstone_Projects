\section{Velocity-Aware Kinematic Control Barrier Shielding}
For an autonomous physical agent moving at approach velocity $v_t$ toward a hard physical boundary $x_{\mathrm{barrier}}$ under measured telemetry transport lag $\tau_{\mathrm{lag}}$ and worst-case invariant deceleration floor $a_{\mathrm{floor}}$, the forward-invariant stopping distance barrier is formulated as:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch13_linear_staleness_barrier}
h(x_t, v_t, w_t) = (x_{\mathrm{barrier}} - x_t) - \left(v_t \tau_{\mathrm{eff}}(w_t) + \frac{v_t^2}{2 a_{\mathrm{floor}}} + d_{\mathrm{margin}}\right) \ge 0,
\end{equation}
where the effective staleness horizon $\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}(w_t) = \tau_{\mathrm{lag}} + 0.35(1 - w_t) + 0.15$ inflates conservatism as observation quality degrades, and the Observation--Reality Integrity weight $w_t \in [0.05, 1.0]$ decays exponentially with both packet age beyond nominal cadence ($20\text{ ms}$) and kinematic Fault Detection and Isolation (FDI) spoofing residual $r_{\mathrm{FDI}}$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch13_oqe_weight}
w_t = \operatorname{clip}\!\left(\exp\!\big(-3.2 \max(0, \tau_{\mathrm{lag}} - 0.02)\big) \cdot \exp\!\big(-2.5 r_{\mathrm{FDI}}\big),\, 0.05,\, 1.0\right).
\end{equation}

\section{100,000-Agent Swarm Under Active Learned Adversary}
To evaluate scalability under worst-case timing attacks, we deployed an adversarial neural network (\texttt{AdversaryNet}) trained online via gradient ascent to observe fleet kinematics $[v_{\mathrm{ego}}, d_{\mathrm{gap}}]$ and inject state-dependent latency spikes (up to $\tau_{\max} = 1,000\text{ ms}$ and $\tau_{\max} = 3,000\text{ ms}$) precisely during lead-vehicle emergency deceleration windows ($-8.0\text{ m/s}^2$).

\paragraph{Mathematical bridge from passive staleness (Eq.~\ref{eq:ch13_linear_staleness_barrier}) to minimax adversarial anticipation (Eq.~\ref{eq:ch13_minimax_barrier}).}
Whereas Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch13_linear_staleness_barrier} inflates stopping distance linearly via $\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}(w_t)$ using the \emph{already-observed} packet age $\tau_{\mathrm{lag}}$, an active adversary can withhold packets abruptly at the exact instant the lead vehicle initiates $-8.0\text{ m/s}^2$ emergency braking---before $\tau_{\mathrm{lag}}$ has accumulated in the receiver timestamp monitor. To guarantee forward invariance against unannounced delay spikes up to worst-case envelope $\tau_{\max}$, we replace the passive linear term $v_t \tau_{\mathrm{eff}}(w_t) + d_{\mathrm{margin}}$ with the sum of an inverse reliability barrier $d_{\mathrm{base}}/w_t$ (which expands hyperbolically as $w_t \to 0.05$) and a \textbf{Minimax Proactive Anticipatory Buffer} $v_t \tau_{\max}$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch13_minimax_barrier}
B_{\mathrm{dyn}}(v_t, w_t) = \frac{v_t^2}{2 a_{\max}} + \frac{d_{\mathrm{base}}}{w_t} + v_t \tau_{\max}.
\end{equation}
Because the anticipatory term $v_t \tau_{\max}$ scales directly with the certified adversarial delay ceiling $\tau_{\max}$, the $\tau_{\max} = 3.0\text{ s}$ extreme-adversary regime enforces a substantially larger pre-braking headway envelope ($100\text{--}180\text{ m}$ initial clearance band) and initiates emergency deceleration earlier in the approach trajectory than both the nominal regime ($\tau_{\max} = 0.02\text{ s}$) and the $1.0\text{ s}$ spike regime ($60\text{--}120\text{ m}$ initial clearance band). Consequently, when agents come to a full stop, the $\tau_{\max} = 3.0\text{ s}$ proactive envelope locks in a larger terminal stopping margin ($55.02\text{ m}$ minimum, $98.46\text{ m}$ mean) than the $1.0\text{ s}$ spike regime ($15.74\text{ m}$ minimum) and nominal cruising ($42.10\text{ m}$ minimum), as shown in Table~\ref{tab:swarm_adversary}.

\section{The K-Bound Epistemic Mind: Knowability Frontier \& Three-Way Conformal Adaptation Gate}

\begin{itemize}

By disagreement-region reduction, $\Delta = R_T(f_0) - R_T(f_a) = 2d(M + \gamma)$ with exact feasible benefit interval $\Delta(\mathcal{C}_\beta) = 2d[\max(-\frac{1}{2}, M-\beta), \min(\frac{1}{2}, M+\beta)]$. The pointwise maximal sound rule commits to \texttt{ADAPT} iff $M > \beta$, \texttt{FREEZE} iff $M < -\beta$, and \texttt{ABSTAIN} throughout the closed unknowable band $|M| \le \beta$, where matched-evidence opposite-sign worlds force $\mathbb{P}(\texttt{ABSTAIN}) \ge 1 - 2\alpha$ and the least valid label-free fibre-wise audit budget satisfies $\Gamma_z(\mathcal{C}_\beta) = \beta$.
