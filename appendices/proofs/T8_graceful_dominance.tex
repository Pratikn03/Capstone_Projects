\paragraph{Theorem T8: Graceful Degradation Dominance with Useful Work.}
Let \(P_G\) be an ORIUS graceful-degradation policy and \(P_U\) an uncontrolled
persistence policy evaluated on the same admissible fault trace. Let \(Z_t^G\)
and \(Z_t^U\) be true-state violation indicators, and let \(W_G,W_U\) be useful
work totals. If \(Z_t^G\le Z_t^U\) for all \(t\) and \(W_G\ge \lambda W_U\) for
\(\lambda\in(0,1]\), then \(P_G\) weakly dominates \(P_U\) in safety while
preserving at least a \(\lambda\)-fraction of useful work.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Summing the pointwise inequality gives
\(\sum_t Z_t^G\le \sum_t Z_t^U\), so the graceful policy has no larger violation
count or violation rate on the paired trace. The second hypothesis is exactly
the useful-work lower bound. These two inequalities are the product-order
dominance relation used by the theorem: safety is weakly improved and work is
retained above the declared threshold.
