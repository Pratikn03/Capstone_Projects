\chapter{Claim-to-Evidence Matrix}
\label{app:claim-evidence}

Every promoted theorem row is represented by a machine-readable result card in
\path{reports/publication/theorem_result_cards/}.  Each card contains:
\[
\texttt{claim\_id},\quad
\texttt{proof\_file},\quad
\texttt{code\_anchor},\quad
\texttt{test\_file},\quad
\texttt{artifact\_path},\quad
\texttt{status},\quad
\texttt{claim\_boundary}.
\]
Artifact hashes are part of the evidence contract.  A theorem cannot be
promoted by prose alone; if the artifact hash or path fails validation, the row
is not publication-ready.

\section{Evidence Families}
The evidence families are certificate horizon and expiry (T5), safety-work
frontier (T8), ambiguity witnesses (T9), boundary lower-bound curves (T10),
Byzantine reliability sweeps (T11Byz), stale-hold growth (Tstale), trajectory
PAC horizon sweeps (T\_trajectory\_PAC), runtime law audits (L1--L4), finite
ambiguity minimax grids (Tminimax), and sensor-ablation witnesses (Tsensor).

\section{Domain Lane}
Battery, AV, and Healthcare provide the current three-domain evidence lane.
T11Byz is required on Battery and optional on AV/Healthcare until those domains
expose multi-channel adversarial telemetry artifacts.
